↘︎ ↖︎ NARRATING THE CRACKS

3D, Moving Images

VIDEO

2022

Using 3D scanning as a storytelling methodology Narrating the Cracks explores the political consequences of climate change in Trentino communities.

Components
↪︎ 3D scanning, procedural modelling

Software
↪︎ Blender, Adobe Premiere, Zephyr 3D

Mentoring
↪︎ Bruno Alves de Almeida, Giuditta Vendrame, Jeroen van de Gruiter, Formafantasma

Narrating the Cracks is a double-channel video essay that question the tight relationship between social administration and environmental conditions of the Southern Alps region.

Using wood as a case study, the project seeks to underline the indissoluble symbiosis between socio and eco-systemical processes. Within the context of the research, climate change covers the dual role of cause and caused in its already started path towards the subversion of contemporary human forms of government.
